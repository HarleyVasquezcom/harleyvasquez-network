Network lines form H + V • 2px stroke • No fills

Philosophy: Dots = ports, Lines = connections

The H and V share a middle link — symbolizing interconnected networks. Designed to read as a patch panel at 16px and as a hero at 4K.

Scalable

Favicon to rack label. Works at 16px favicon, embossed on top plates, stitched on polo, watermarked on photos.

Transparent

Any background. White knockout, pure vector stroke, dark and light versions for documentation and hardware.

System

Cisco-blue + navy trust. Primary #00898A for rack tags, accent #2ED6D8 for link status LEDs in renders.

Typography

Wide tracking caps. Lexend 600 for headings, DM Sans for cable schedules — modern sans, all caps.
